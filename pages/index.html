About

Salim Reggieray is an alternative rock artist based out of Bellingham, WA. He draws from a variety of influences such as Muse, Jeff Buckley, Mazzy Star, Alabama Shakes, and Grouper among others. Their sound is defined by a blend of liquid atmospheric arrangements, contemporary rock riffs and catchy lyrics.

  • Salim Reggieray // Guitar & Vocals

  • Colby Mcglinn // Bass

  • Freddy Mercury // Drums

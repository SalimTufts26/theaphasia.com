About

Salim Reggieray is an alternative rock artist, producer, and audio engineer based out of Bellingham, WA. He draws from a variety of influences such as Muse, Jeff Buckley, Mazzy Star, Alabama Shakes, and Grouper. His sound is defined by a blend of liquidy atmospheric arrangements, contemporary rock riffs and catchy lyrics.
